When They Vanish

Every year, hundreds and sometimes thousands of people attempting to migrate go missing. Migrants that are lost in translation between dangerous migration routes, bordeers, policies and legal systems that fail to protect them. As the global trend of missing migrants increases, it becomes paramount to document and communicate the magnitude of this issue, so to honor the memory of those who vanished and take a first step towards creating a more accountable migration system.

Each = one missing migrant
